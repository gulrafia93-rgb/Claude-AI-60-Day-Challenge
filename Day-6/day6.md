**Day 6 – AI Resume Optimization | 60 Days AI Challenge**

**Overview**

Welcome to Day 6 of my 60 Days AI Challenge.

In this project, I explored how Artificial Intelligence can help optimize resumes by improving content quality, identifying missing keywords, enhancing formatting, and increasing ATS (Applicant Tracking System) compatibility.

The goal is to understand how AI can assist job seekers in creating more professional and recruiter-friendly resumes.
