# Day 30 – Supply Chain Builder | ABTalks 60-Day Coding Challenge 

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive Supply Chain Builder that helps users understand and optimize supply chain operations through practical business decisions and real-time performance metrics.

## Features

* Random Company Generator
* Supplier Strategy Selection
* Factory Location Planning
* Warehouse Strategy
* Transportation Method Selection
* Inventory Optimization
* Live Business Metrics
* Overall Supply Chain Score
* Performance Dashboard
* Replay Functionality

## Technologies Used

* HTML
* CSS
* React (CDN)
* JavaScript
* Babel JSX

## Key Learning

> Successful supply chain optimization requires balancing cost, delivery speed, operational risk, sustainability, and customer satisfaction. Small strategic decisions can significantly improve overall business performance.

## Outcome

Built a complete browser-based enterprise simulation that demonstrates supply chain optimization through interactive decision-making, real-time business metrics, and performance analysis.
