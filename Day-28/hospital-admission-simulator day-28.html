⚖️ Observation Status — CMS Compliance Notice
CMS 2-Midnight Rule applies — Observation status carries different cost-sharing, SNF eligibility, and billing rules than Inpatient. Medicare patients are responsible for Part B cost-sharing rather than Part A deductible under Observation. SNF benefit is not triggered. Medicare patients require written MOON (Medicare Outpatient Observation Notice) notification within 36 hours of Observation placement or before discharge, whichever is earlier.
