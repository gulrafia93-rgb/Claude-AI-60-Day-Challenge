## Day 16 – Build Your First Stock Research Skill

A reusable AI workflow that turns repetitive stock research prompts into a structured, automated analysis system.
---
## Overview

This project demonstrates how to convert long stock analysis prompts into a reusable AI Skill, enabling consistent and efficient company research without rewriting prompts every time.

--
## Key Features
1. Reusable stock research workflow
2. Structured fundamental analysis framework
3. Valuation & profitability evaluation
4. Growth and risk assessment
5. Productivity-focused automation
6. How It Works

Instead of manually prompting AI each time, a predefined “Skill” is used to automatically analyze companies using a fixed structure:
Business → Financials → Valuation → Growth → Risks → Summary
---
## Benefits
Saves time on repetitive prompts
Ensures consistent analysis output
Improves research quality and clarity
Scalable for multiple stocks

---
## Learning Outcome

Learned how to design reusable AI workflows that transform prompt engineering into a systematic research tool.
