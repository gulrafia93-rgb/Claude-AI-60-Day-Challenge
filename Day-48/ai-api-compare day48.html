Approach

Every data point below was pulled directly from each provider's own documentation, pricing pages, or rate-limit pages (fetched live). Where an official page did not publish a fixed, citable number — this happened for OpenAI's rate-limit page (blocked bot detection on direct fetch) and Google's exact free-tier RPM/RPD figures (Google's own docs point to a live dashboard rather than a static table) — that value is built from multiple independent third-party trackers instead, and is explicitly flagged EST in the detail view for each provider card. No numbers were invented; estimate flags mean "sourced from secondary aggregators, not a first-party static table," not "guessed."

Conflicts encountered & how they were resolved

  • OpenAI free tier: Secondary sources disagree on whether a $5 signup credit still exists in 2026 — some say it was discontinued in mid-2025, others report it still appears "inconsistently" by region/promotion. We treated this as "no reliably guaranteed free credit" for scoring purposes, since a developer can't plan around an inconsistent grant.
  • Gemini free-tier RPM: Third-party trackers report different numbers for the same models (e.g. Flash at 10 RPM in one source, Gemini 3 Flash at 10 RPM / 1,500 RPD in another) as Google has changed quotas multiple times in the past year (including a reported Dec 2025 quota cut). We used the most recent (mid-2026) figures and flagged the range as an estimate.
  • Cohere pricing page: Cohere's own /pricing page does not render current Command A / Command R+ per-token rates in static HTML — it shows legacy model pricing only. We cross-verified current Command A pricing ($2.50 / $10.00 per million tokens) across four independent trackers before using it, and flagged it as third-party-confirmed rather than pulled from a first-party pricing table.
  • Latency: None of the four providers publish standardized, comparable latency benchmarks. Scores here reflect each vendor's own stated speed-tier positioning (e.g. "fastest, most cost-efficient" model in a family) plus one third-party benchmark for Cohere, not a controlled head-to-head test. Treat the latency dimension as directional, not a lab measurement.

What "estimate" means throughout this tool

Anywhere you see EST next to a number, it means that figure could not be confirmed on a first-party static page at the time of writing and instead reflects a synthesis of independently-reported third-party data (rate-limit trackers, pricing calculators, or benchmark sites) current as of mid-2026. Figures without that flag were read directly from the provider's own documentation.

Data current as of July 2026. AI API pricing, free tiers, and rate limits change frequently — always confirm against the provider's live pricing page before committing. This tool reflects a synthesis of research, not financial or technical advice.
