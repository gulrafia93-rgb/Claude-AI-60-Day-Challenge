Shift Briefing

You're now Head of Operations.

For the next 3 minutes, disruptions will hit your global supply chain in real time — port delays, supplier failures, demand spikes, and more. Every alert costs you time. Choose the right response before the clock runs out, or the KPIs pay the price.

  • Resolve fast: each alert has its own countdown. Let it expire and it auto-escalates against you.
  • Pick wisely: some actions fix the root cause, some are quick patches, some are the wrong tool for the job.
  • Delay carries risk: postponing a decision often means it comes back worse.
  • Watch the cost: every fix has a price — the best operators protect revenue without burning the budget.
Reference

How the Tower works

  • KPIs — Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health and Transport Efficiency all run 0–100%. Operating Cost is an index (lower is better). Revenue Protected accumulates in dollars. Score is your overall performance readout.
  • Alerts — arrive continuously and speed up as the shift progresses. Each shows a priority (Critical / High / Medium), a countdown bar, and its likely business impact if ignored.
  • Actions — every alert offers 2–3 context-specific responses plus Ignore and Delay Decision. The best response addresses the root cause; weaker ones patch the symptom or cost more than they're worth.
  • Consequences — Ignoring or letting an alert expire always hurts KPIs, cost, and score. Delaying buys time but the issue returns sharper, with a smaller window to fix it.
  • Grading — at the end of the shift you're graded A+ through D based on your KPI health and total score.
