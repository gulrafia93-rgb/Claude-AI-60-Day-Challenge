#  Day 8: Personal Environmental Health Analyzer

## Project Overview
Welcome to Day 8 of the 60-Day AI Challenge! Today's project is an interactive, full-stack inspired **Personal Environmental Health Analyzer Dashboard**. It bridges the gap between raw environmental data (AQI, PM2.5, PM10, Water Quality Index) and its direct biological/physiological impacts on human health—specifically targeting respiration, sleep quality, haircare, and skincare.

The application automatically centers focus on **Nowshera, Pakistan** and provides real-time cross-regional data comparisons with surrounding major urban hubs using advanced interactive visualizations.

---

## Key Features

### 1.  Interactive Control Deck & Filters
* **City Selector:** Toggle seamlessly between **Nowshera**, Peshawar, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Lahore, and Karachi.
* **Component Filters:** Dynamically isolate specific data tracks like Air Quality Index (AQI), PM2.5 micro-particles, and Water Quality scores on the fly.

### 2.  Cross-Analysis Visualizations
* Powered by **Chart.js**, featuring smooth premium animations inside a dark-mode theme.
* Fully responsive layout that scales elegantly from mobile screens to wide desktop monitors.

### 3.  Deep Physiological & Dermatological Breakdown
* **Air Pollutant Burden:** Analyzes how current particle counts compress VO2 Max, disrupt sleep architecture, and induce alveolar stress.
* **Hydrochemistry Analysis:** Connects local water mineralization, hard-water scaling (Calcium/Magnesium), and pH levels to hair fall, scalp dryness, and skin barrier breakdown.

### 4. Localized Report Card
* Assigns weighted **Environmental Health Scores (0-100)**.
* Generates academic-style grading scales (**A–F**) for both fluid and atmospheric ecosystems based on June 2026 climate metrics.

### 5. Targeted Mitigation Directives
* Dynamic, real-time advice panels that swap instructions out based on the active city's threat level—providing explicit protocols for **Indoor Air**, **Skin Defense**, and **Water Treatment**.
