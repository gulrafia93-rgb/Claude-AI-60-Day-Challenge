# Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator | ABTalks 60-Day Coding Challenge

## Project Overview

Built an AI-powered Shark Tank Simulator that allows users to pitch startup ideas and receive feedback from virtual investors.

## Features

* Startup Pitch Submission
* AI Investor Panel
* Interactive Question & Answer Rounds
* Startup Scoring System
* Investment Decision Engine
* Valuation & Funding Recommendations
* Leaderboard System
* Pitch Report Generation

## AI Judges

* Venture Capitalist
* Founder
* Customer
* Angel Investor

## Evaluation Criteria

* Market Potential
* Innovation
* Business Model
* Execution
* Investment Worthiness

## Key Learning

> Investors fund businesses that solve real problems with scalable and sustainable models.

## Outcome

Developed a complete business simulation application that demonstrates startup evaluation, investor decision-making, and business strategy concepts.
