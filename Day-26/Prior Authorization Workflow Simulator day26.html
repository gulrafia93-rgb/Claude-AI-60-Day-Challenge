Key Learnings

  • Document every failed medication attempt with start dates, dosages, side effects, and stop reasons.
  • Specialty drugs are authorized through the PBM, not the medical insurer.
  • Biosimilar conversion requests from payers may be legally challengeable if the prescriber documents medical necessity for the original biologic.
